Goji-B builds reliable electronic modules and publishes practical Zephyr RTOS sample code that users can run, verify, and adapt.

## Why this matters

- Buyers need confidence before hardware purchases.
- Learners need complete examples, not broken snippets.
- Investors need a clear, credible product story with execution evidence.

## Working policy

- Samples are treated as production-grade references.
- Zephyr source remains in dedicated Git repositories.
- This site documents intent, use cases, versions, and links.
